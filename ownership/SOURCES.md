## South African copyright background

[Creative Expressions — an introduction to copyright for small and medium enterprises](https://www.thedtic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/publication-CCRD_Creative_Expressions.pdf), hosted by South Africa's Department of Trade, Industry and Competition.

The guide distinguishes protected expression from underlying ideas and discusses authorship, ownership and computer programs. It is general background, not a determination of the rights in this AI-assisted app or a substitute for checking current law and relevant agreements.

[Copyright Amendment Act 125 of 1992, section 1(d)](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201409/act125of1992.pdf), official government copy. The amendment adds definitions concerning arrangements for computer-generated works and control over making a computer program. It is a historical statutory source, not a consolidated review of every later amendment or a ruling on modern AI output. SOURCE-ORIGINS.md records the user's direction for assessment against relevant law; it does not certify that every generated part qualifies.
